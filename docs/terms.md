# Terms of Service

Gemini Exporter Stats is a maintainer-only automation used to retrieve read-only, aggregate Google Analytics data for the Gemini Exporter Chrome Web Store listing.

The service is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind.

Use of Gemini Exporter Stats is limited to the project maintainer and is solely for maintaining public aggregate project statistics.

Gemini Exporter Stats does not provide services to Chrome extension users and does not grant third parties access to Google Analytics data.
